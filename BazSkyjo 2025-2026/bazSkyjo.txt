# BazSkyjo

## Flex Friday
Baz Skyjo is my 2025-2026 Flex Friday project at Baxter Academy.
Every Friday, I spent the majority of the day working on this project, and presented it to the school after it's completion at the end of the year.

## Project Description
Skyjo is a strategy based card game played between two or more players.
The goal of the game is to end the round with the a lower score than your opponent.
My program is a dueling DQN nueral network that will play against the user.
It uses three internal layers of 512 neurons, with a trunkated layer of 64 neurons feeding into the output.
It has an average score of 14 in training, which is on par with human players.
