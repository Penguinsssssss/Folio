# Todd Quoridor

## Flex Friday
Todd Quoridor is my 2024-2025 Flex Friday project at Baxter Academy.
Every Friday, I spent the majority of the day working on this project, and presented it to the school after it's completion at the end of the year.

## Project Description
Quoridor is a turn based strategy board game played between two players.
The goal of the game is to move your pawn to the other side of the board before the opponent moves their pawn to yours.
My program is a MiniMax function with a customizable depth up to 5.
My program uses a Dijkstra Algorithm as it's evaluation function
My program uses Alpha-Beta pruning to reduce search times
My program has a winrate of 19-2 win/loss against humans
